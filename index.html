Car maintenance shouldn't require sitting in an uncomfortable waiting room or wondering whether you're being sold something you don't need. Questions

Do I need to supply anything?

For the $50 service-only option, yes: the correct oil and filter. For full-service appointments, I bring them.

How long does an oil change take?

Most appointments take about 30–60 minutes depending on the vehicle and access.

What happens to the old oil?

Used motor oil is collected and taken for proper recycling.

Can I stay inside?

Yes. Once access to the vehicle is arranged, you don't need to sit outside watching the entire service.
